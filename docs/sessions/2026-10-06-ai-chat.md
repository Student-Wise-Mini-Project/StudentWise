## What was built

- **Backend**
  - `chat_conversations` and `chat_messages`. The migration is
    `7bd0ad0e9592`, with the duplicated CHECK constraint removed by hand, as
    in the Gmail migration.
  - `app/ai/chat.py` holds the one model call, which tests replace.
  - `services/chat_tools.py` defines nine read-only tools over existing
    services.
  - `services/chat_service.py` runs the tool loop and saves the conversation.
  - `api/chat.py` has five endpoints.
  - `ConversationForOwner` in `core/deps.py`.
- **Frontend**
  - `features/chat/`: a list of your conversations and a conversation
    screen, reached from a card on Insights.
  - The question shows at once with "Looking at your numbers…".
  - Each answer says what it looked at ("Looked at balances · unusual
    expenses").
  - A failed question keeps its text for "Try again".
  - Deleting a conversation asks first.
  - English and Hebrew catalogues.
- **Tests:** 40 backend tests (`tests/api/test_chat.py`, with Claude played by
  a scripted fake) and 10 frontend tests (`features/chat/chat.test.tsx`).

## Decisions

- **Read-only tools, each a call to a tested service.** The assistant never
  adds money up itself: balances come from `balance_service`, and the
  fallback is the Ask screen's guarded Text-to-SQL. It can explain a balance
  but not move one. Asked to add an expense, it points at the + button.
- **Only questions and answers are stored**, not tool calls. Each turn
  re-runs its tools, so "and now?" never reasons from numbers that have since
  changed. `tools_used` keeps the names, so the screen can say what was looked
  at.
- **Nothing is saved unless an answer exists.** The model loop runs before
  any row is written. A 503 leaves no orphan question behind, and the screen
  gives the text back.
- **Private conversations: 404, not 403.** A 403 would tell a flatmate that
  you have been asking about the group's money.
- **The documented manual loop, not the beta tool runner.** Tools need the
  request's session and group, tests replace one function, and the project
  avoids beta APIs. The model's own content blocks are sent back untouched,
  because adaptive thinking requires it.
- **Adaptive thinking on `claude-sonnet-5`** (`CHAT_MODEL`), one retry, a
  60-second timeout, and at most 6 rounds.

## Tested on the real model

- **Balances and the settle-up plan:** matched the app to the cent.
- **"Electricity overall":** ₪4,055.66. That includes the Hebrew-titled bill
  imported from Gmail, found because the prompt says to search titles in both
  languages.
- **The ₪1,244 August bill:** flagged.
- **"Add an expense for me":** politely refused.
- **Browser (Edge, phone size):** 14/14 checks pass, English and Hebrew,
  including right-to-left layout and no money moved.

The prompt was tightened three times from what the real model did:

1. Category codes appeared in a Hebrew reply ("UTILITIES"). It now names
   them in words.
2. A stray English "so" appeared in a Hebrew reply. The reply is now
   entirely in its language.
3. "נועה צריכה לשלם" guessed Noa's gender from her name. There are now
   examples for other people, not just "you". After that: 4/4 Hebrew replies
   gender-neutral, with no stray English.
