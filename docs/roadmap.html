The honest read. The app works end to end. You can sign in, create a flat, add flatmates and weights, add an expense in any of the four split modes, see the balances move, settle up, comment, attach a receipt, read your alerts, see where the money goes, install it to an iOS home screen — and run the whole thing in Hebrew. Epic 9 is 16 of 17: you can ask a question in your own words, and an unusual bill or a double payment is flagged. Only the APK is left.

The backend has been finished for a week. Epics 1–6 are complete, and the Ask screen is measured: right on 99% of a question set in English and Hebrew. What remains is somebody else’s — the AI ingestion module — or is waiting on a person rather than on code.

So the constraint is two people who still cannot see the code. Nothing here is short of engineering time. Both of the blockers below are a few minutes of somebody’s attention, and both have been on this page for days.
