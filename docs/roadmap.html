The honest read. A week ago the problem was that a substantial API had no interface. That is no longer true: Epic 9 went from nothing to nine of twelve missions — scaffold, typed client, auth, groups, expenses, the split editor, balances, settle-up, charts and the PWA. You can log in, add an expense split four different ways, watch the balances move, record a payment, and install the whole thing to an iOS home screen.

The backend is finished and has been for days. Epics 1, 2, 3 and 6 are complete; Epic 4 has one mission left and it is blocked on a key. What remains is somebody else’s — the AI ingestion module — or is waiting on a person rather than on code.

Which is now the real constraint. Two things are blocked on a human decision, not on engineering time, and neither takes more than a few minutes to unblock.
